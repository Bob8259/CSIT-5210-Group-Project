1. **Full Member Participation:** All members in your group should give a presentation and ask questions to the other group assigned.

## 2. Format & Interactive Requirements

- **Delivery Mode:** Face-to-face oral presentation.
- **Q&A Responsiveness:** In the assigned presentation time slot, give an oral presentation and be ready to answer questions asked by students and the instructor.
- **Questioning Task:** In the assigned time slot, each group will be assigned to ask questions to another group (on the day of presentation). **Each member in this group must ask at least one question for the other group.**
- **Student Presentation Order Form (DOC):** Download the form from [Canvas Link](https://canvas.ust.hk/courses/72073/files/12446916?wrap=1), fill in presenter details and order, and submit the filled form to the submission site before the assigned presentation time slot. This form helps the instructor identify each presenter clearly to allocate individual scores.
